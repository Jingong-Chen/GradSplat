# re10k_dining_room

Six input frames of the RealEstate10K test scene `18502a6651367e71`, the scene of Figure 1 of the paper (narrow protocol), with the frame numbers 0, 6, 13, 25, 36, 49 of the evaluation index `re10k_narrow_volsplat.json`. The images are the frames of the test set as distributed by pixelSplat, unchanged; `cameras.json` holds the camera of each frame (camera-to-world, OpenCV axes, and the pixel intrinsics of the image).

RealEstate10K: Zhou et al., *Stereo Magnification: Learning View Synthesis using Multiplane Images*, SIGGRAPH 2018, https://google.github.io/realestate10k/. Its camera data is licensed by Google LLC under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. The frames come from the YouTube videos that the dataset lists and are included here only as examples for noncommercial research.
